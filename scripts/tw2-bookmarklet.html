TW2 Route Extractor (Fine Grid)

Copy this script, paste into the TW2 console. Scans 5km grid, finds all routes.

  1. Go to TW2 map (logged in)
  2. Open DevTools (F12) > Console
  3. Click "Copy" below, paste in console, press Enter
  4. Wait 3-5 minutes
  5. Replace src/data/tw2-routes.json with the downloaded file
