How it works

This scans the Durham/Yorkshire area using WMS GetFeatureInfo (the same request TW2 makes when you click the map). It collects route GUIDs and TWUIDs from each response. Takes 2-5 minutes depending on density.

Instructions

  1. Go to TW2 map (logged in)
  2. Drag the green button to your bookmarks bar
  3. Click it - watch the progress bar
  4. When done, move tw2-routes.json to src/data/tw2-routes.json
